The Dragon's Shadow

A Tale of 500AC

The year is 500AC — the Age of Shadows. For three hundred years, the Superlord Malachar has ruled the land with an iron fist, his power drawn from the ancient dragons he has enslaved.

Tonight, his darkness has come to your home.

Flames consume the village. Screams echo through the night. And from the sky, a dragon — eyes glowing with malevolent purple fire — descends upon you all. In its claws, struggling and crying out, is the one person you hold most dear: your spouse.

The dragon vanishes into the night, carrying everything you love toward the Superlord's fortress in the Shadowpeak Mountains.

Your journey begins now.
