The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VIII - The Duel of Wills

Step 8 of 8

Purple light hangs in the air between you and the throne — a lattice of light, ten columns wide and twenty rows high. Ancient runes circle its border, and pieces of cursed stone drift through it, dropping into place with a sound like cracking bone.

"Your battlefield," Malachar murmurs, settling back into his throne. "Twenty lines before me, and you live. Fill your own grid, hero. I am very old, and I have all the time in the world."

This is no duel of swords. Malachar strikes at your grid itself: every few moments a wave of dark stone crashes down into your rows and clogs them. But the game rewards the bold — clear four lines at once and you send the darkness straight back into his grid.

Behind him the dragon watches, the Amulet of Domination pulsing against its chains. Beside the throne your spouse is still trapped. Win, and both are yours. Lose, and the shadows keep everything.

Two grids. One amulet. Play.
