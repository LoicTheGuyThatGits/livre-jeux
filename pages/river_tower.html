Chapter II - The Watcher's Isle

Step 3 of 8

You row to the island. The tower is older than the Superlord — Dragon Lord architecture, built when dragons ruled freely.

Inside: a scrying pool showing the fortress throne room. Malachar sits on his throne. The dragon is chained. Your spouse is in the cage. Right now.

A warding amulet hangs on the wall — crafted by the First Dragons to resist the Amulet of Domination. You take it. It burns cold against your chest.

A ghostly voice: "The chain binds both ways. Break the amulet, free the dragon. But the dragon's freedom breaks the cage. Choose what you value more."

The tower shakes. The lake stirs. Something vast rises from the depths.

What do you do?
