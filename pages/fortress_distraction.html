Chapter VI - The False Alarm

Step 6.5 of 8

You set the alchemical charges at the fortress's western powder store — stolen from the goblins, stabilized with prayer and luck. The fuse burns slow.

You retreat to the eastern wall. The explosion thunders — stone screams, the ground heaves. Guards pour toward the breach. The eastern postern is unguarded.

You slip inside, heart pounding. Corridors empty. Stairs climb. You ascend toward the throne room, the distraction holding.

Then — a silent alarm. A pulse of purple light races through the stones. The Superlord knew.

Undead cut you off ahead. Behind, the returning guards. The distraction was a trap. You are caught in the pincer.

What do you do?
