The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter IV - The Loose Stone

Step 4.5 of 8

Midnight. The storehouse sits at the village edge, a heavy padlock on its door. You pick it with a wire from your pack — clean entry.

Inside: barrels of salted meat, grain sacks, rope coils, lantern oil. You fill your pack efficiently, professionally. A map case catches your eye — patrol routes, guard rotations, a hidden mountain path marked in red ink.

You memorize the path. A loose stone in the doorway grinds as you leave.

"HALT!"

Torches ignite. Crossbows click. You sprint for the village gate, an arrow grazing your thigh. The wound slows you. The gate looms — and slams shut in your face.

You climb the wall, drop outside, and limp into the night. The map is in your mind. The mountain awaits. But the bleeding won't stop.

What do you do?
