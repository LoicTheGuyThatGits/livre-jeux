The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter I - The Hunter's Path

Step 1.5 of 8

Your mind races, but your hands move with purpose. You dash into your home — or what remains of it — and grab the essentials: a sturdy sword from above the hearth, a worn leather pack, dried meat, a waterskin, and a coil of rope.

Your neighbor, Old Marta, presses a healing salve into your palm. "For the road," she whispers, her eyes wet. "The mountains are cruel to the unprepared."

You sling the pack over your shoulder, the sword's weight familiar at your hip. The dragon's trail is still fresh — a scorched path through the treeline leading toward the Whispering Woods.

You have supplies. You have a weapon. You have a chance.

The forest swallows you whole as you begin the pursuit in earnest.

How do you track the dragon?
