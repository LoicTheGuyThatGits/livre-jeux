The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Maw of Earth

Step 2.5 of 8

The cave mouth yawns before you like a wound in the earth. Cold, stale air rushes out, carrying the scent of old blood and something older. You light a torch and step inside.

The tunnel descends steeply. Bioluminescent fungi cast eerie green light on walls scarred by ancient claws. Deeper now. The air grows thick with the smell of goblin — sweat, rot, and crude cooking fires.

You hear voices ahead. Dozens of them. A goblin warren, not a simple camp. This cave system honeycombs the entire mountain range.

A goblin sentry spots you. The alarm goes up. There is no retreat — only the dark ahead, or the swarm behind.

How do you survive?
