The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Whispering Water

Step 2.5 of 8

The river path follows a swift mountain stream, its waters dark and cold. Mist clings to the surface, masking your scent — and your vision. The current is deceptively strong, fed by glacial melt from the Shadowpeaks.

You walk the rocky bank for hours. The dragon's trail crosses and re-crosses the water, suggesting it flew low here, perhaps drinking. You find a single scale caught on a riverside bush — obsidian, warm to the touch, pulsing faintly.

Night falls. The mist thickens. Strange lights dance on the water — will-o'-wisps or something worse. The river widens into a black lake. In its center, a small island holds a ruined tower.

A boat rots at the water's edge. The far shore continues toward the mountains. The tower might hold shelter — or answers.

Which way do you go?
