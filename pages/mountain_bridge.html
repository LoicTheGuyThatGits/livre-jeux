The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter V - The Swaying Thread

Step 5.5 of 8

The rope bridge spans a chasm deep enough to swallow the world. Frayed hemp cables. Rotting wooden slats. It sways violently in the wind, a living thing trying to shake you off.

You step onto it. The bridge groans. Slat cracks — your boot punches through. You catch the cable, heart in throat, and haul yourself up.

Halfway. The wind redoubles. A cable snaps. The bridge tilts, dumping you toward the void.

You catch the remaining cable with one hand. Your pack slips, falls, disappears into the dark. Supplies gone. Rope gone. The blessed blade (if you had it) gone.

You climb hand-over-hand, muscles screaming. You reach the far side, haul yourself up, and lie gasping on cold stone. Alive. Barely equipped. But the fortress gates are right there.

What now?
