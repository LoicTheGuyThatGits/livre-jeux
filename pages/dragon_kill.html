Chapter VII - The Dragon's Fall

Step 7.5 of 8

You drive your sword into the dragon's heart. The great beast shudders, a sound like breaking mountains escaping its throat. The purple fire in its eyes vanishes — replaced by gold, warm and grateful.

"Thank you," it whispers, a voice like wind through ancient pines. "Three hundred years... I am free."

The dragon collapses. The amulet around Malachar's neck screams — cracks spiderwebbing across its surface.

But the cage holding your spouse — it was bound to the dragon's life. As the beast dies, the cage dissolves. Your spouse falls free — and does not rise.

You catch them. No breath. No heartbeat. The dragon's freedom cost their life. Malachar screams as the amulet shatters. You kneel in the throne room, holding your dead love, victorious and utterly destroyed.

What now?
