The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Shale Slide

Step 3 of 8

The hidden trail is treacherous — loose shale on a sheer cliff face, twenty feet above the goblin camp. One kicked stone means discovery. Death.

You move like a shadow. Three points of contact. Test each hold. The goblins feast below, unaware. Their fire crackles. They argue over a haunch of stolen mutton.

A section gives way. You drop — catch a root — swing — land silent on a ledge. Heart hammering. Below, a goblin looks up, scratches its head, goes back to eating.

You clear the camp. The trail continues upward, merging with the main path above the amphitheater. You're past the blockade. Unseen. Unhurt.

The mountain opens before you. The fortress looms closer. But you notice something the goblins missed — a faint glow in a crevice halfway up. A warding stone?
