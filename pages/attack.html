The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter I - The Night of Fire

Step 1 of 8

The night air is thick with smoke and terror. Your village — your home — is engulfed in flames. The screams of your neighbors echo through the darkness, mingling with the terrible roar of the beast that has descended upon you.

And then you see it. A dragon, its scales gleaming like molten obsidian in the firelight, its eyes burning with an unnatural purple glow. In its massive claws, struggling and crying out your name, is your spouse.

The dragon beats its wings and rises into the night sky, carrying everything you love toward the Shadowpeak Mountains — toward the fortress of Superlord Malachar.

Your heart pounds. Your hands tremble. The dragon is already disappearing into the distance. You must act — now.

What do you do?
