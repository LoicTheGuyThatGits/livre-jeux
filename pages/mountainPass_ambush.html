The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Mountain's Wrath

Step 3 of 8

You spot the unstable overhang above the goblin camp — a massive slab of fractured granite, held by prayer and friction. Your rope and pitons can bring it down.

You work fast, quiet. Anchor the rope. Wedge the pitons. Saw the last supporting roots. The goblins below never look up.

You pull. The mountain groans. The slab shifts. Then falls — a thousand tons of stone, silent at first, then a thunder that shakes your teeth.

The camp vanishes. Buried. Crushed. Silent. The pass is clear — but the debris blocks the path entirely. You'll have to climb the rubble, or find another way.

In the silence after, you hear it: dragon wings. The beast circles above, drawn by the violence. It sees you. The purple eyes lock onto yours.

The dragon approaches. What do you do?
