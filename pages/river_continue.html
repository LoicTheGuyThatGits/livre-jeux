The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Long Walk

Step 3 of 8

You follow the river bank. The mist never lifts. The water runs black and cold. Strange shapes move beneath the surface — things that should not be.

Days pass. The river widens, slows. You find a fisher's hut — abandoned, but stocked. Dried fish. A sound boat. A journal: "The river feeds the fortress. The fortress feeds the river. Do not cross at the lake."

You take the boat. The hut's supplies. The journal's warning.

The lake looms ahead. The ruined tower on its island. The far shore leads to the mountain path. But the journal warns of something in the water.

How do you cross the lake?
