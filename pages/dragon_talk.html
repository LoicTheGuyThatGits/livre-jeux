The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VII - The Forked Tongue

Step 7.5 of 8

You speak to the dragon. Not at it — to it. You tell it of the skies it once ruled. Of the mate it lost to the Superlord's treachery. Of the eggs smashed in the nest. You speak of freedom.

The purple fire in its eyes flares. The amulet pulses violently. Malachar laughs. "Words? You think words break three hundred years of binding?"

The dragon's maw opens. Purple flame erupts — not at you, but at the chains binding it. The fire burns with the amulet's own power, turned inward.

But the amulet's hold is too strong. The flames curve back, consuming the dragon — and you. The fire is cold, not hot. It burns the soul.

You fall, consciousness fading. The last thing you hear is the dragon's roar — not of rage, but of sorrow. Some chains cannot be broken with words alone.

Your final moment?
