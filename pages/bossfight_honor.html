Chapter VIII - The Duel of Equals

Step 8 of 8

The lattice manifests — but this time, both grids start empty. No handicap. No head start. Pure skill.

Malachar bows slightly. "A fair fight. I have not had one in centuries. Thank you, hero."

The pieces fall. He plays with surgical precision — T-spins, perfect clears, combos that sing. But you match him, move for move. The audience of one dragon watches in silence.

At eighteen lines each, he pauses. Looks at you. "You fight beautifully. Your spouse is proud. Your village would be proud. Even I... am proud."

The final exchange. Two grids. One winner. The amulet waits.

How does this end?
