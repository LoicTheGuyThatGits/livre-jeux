The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter I - The Fool's Errand

Step 1.5 of 8

You sprint through the burning streets, heedless of the flames licking at your heels. The dragon's massive wings beat the air above, each downstroke a thunderclap that shakes the very ground. Your spouse's cries grow fainter with every passing second.

"Wait!" you scream, your voice raw. "I'm coming!"

But the dragon does not wait. It banks toward the Shadowpeak Mountains, a dark silhouette against the star-strewn sky. You run until your lungs burn and your legs threaten to give out, but the distance only grows.

Hours pass. Then days. You chase a shadow across plains and through forests, surviving on berries and rainwater. The trail goes cold. The dragon was never meant to be caught on foot.

You collapse at the foot of the mountains, broken in body and spirit. The Superlord's fortress looms above, unreachable. Your spouse is gone. The darkness has won.

What now?
