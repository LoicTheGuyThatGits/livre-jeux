You charge the main gate, sword raised, a scream tearing from your throat. The undead guards don't flinch. They form a shield wall — ancient steel, unbreakable discipline.

Your blade strikes a shield. The impact jars your arms to the shoulder. Another guard's spear takes you in the thigh. You kill him, but three more fill the gap.

You fight like a demon. Five fall. Ten. Twenty. But they are legion. A mace crushes your ribs. A sword opens your back. You fall to your knees, surrounded by the patient dead.

The gate looms behind them, unbreached. Your vision dims. The last thing you see is a purple glow from the throne room windows.
