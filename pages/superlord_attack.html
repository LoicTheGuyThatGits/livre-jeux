The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VII - The Futile Strike

Step 7.5 of 8

You lunge for the Amulet of Domination — a desperate strike at the source of all evil. Your blade sings through air thick with dark power.

Malachar doesn't move. Doesn't blink. He simply raises one pale hand.

A wave of purple force catches you mid-air. You fly backward, slamming into the throne room's stone pillars. Ribs shatter. Vision swims. Your sword clatters from nerveless fingers.

"Predictable," he murmurs, rising gracefully. He steps to you, looming. The amulet pulses. "Every hero thinks the direct blow will work. Every hero dies the same way."

He places a foot on your chest. The dragon watches. Your spouse screams. Darkness takes you.

Your final thought?
