Chapter II - The High Road

Step 2.5 of 8

The mountain pass winds upward through jagged rocks, the path narrow enough that two people cannot walk abreast. To your left, the mountain wall rises sheer. To your right, a deadly drop into mist-shrouded valleys.

The air grows thin. Your breath comes in ragged gasps. The dragon's trail is clear here — scorch marks on the rocks, the distinctive sulfurous scent of its passage.

Ahead, the pass widens into a natural amphitheater. Smoke rises from a goblin camp nestled in the bowl-shaped depression. They block the only way forward.

You crouch behind a boulder, watching. There are more than you hoped — at least twenty. But you also see something else: a side trail skirting the camp, half-hidden by loose shale.

How do you proceed?
