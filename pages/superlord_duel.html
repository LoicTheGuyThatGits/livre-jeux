Chapter VII - The Formal Challenge

Step 7.5 of 8

"I challenge you to a formal duel of wills," you declare. "Honor against honor. No cheating. No head start."

Malachar's eyebrows rise. A long silence. Then laughter — rich, warm, genuine. "Honor. From a hero. How... refreshing."

He waves a hand. The lattice forms. But his grid remains empty. "Very well. No head start. We begin as equals. May the better will win."

The first pieces drop simultaneously. You realize with a jolt — he's good. Three hundred years of practice. But he's playing fair.

The honorable duel begins. Pure skill against ancient mastery.

How do you play the honorable duel?
