The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter III - The Silent Path

Step 3.5 of 8

You wait. Hours pass. The goblins feast, then drink, then brawl. One by one, they collapse into stuporous sleep around their dying fire. The sentries nod, then slump.

You move like smoke. Booted feet on stone — silent. Breath held — invisible. You slip between sleeping forms, close enough to smell their foul breath.

A goblin mutters in its sleep and rolls over — directly onto your foot.

You don't breathe. The creature smacks its lips, smelling meat in its dreams, and rolls away. Your heart hammers. One more step. Another. You're past them. The pass opens ahead.

You descend toward the village unscathed, unseen, whole.
