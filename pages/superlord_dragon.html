The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VII - The Scaled Prisoner

Step 7.5 of 8

You ignore Malachar. You ignore the amulet. You walk straight to the dragon.

The beast's eyes burn purple — the amulet's chains made visible. It snarls, straining against bindings that have held it for centuries. But beneath the magical fire, you see intelligence. Pain. A soul.

"I'm not here for the Superlord," you say, voice steady. "I'm here for you."

The dragon pauses. The purple fire flickers. For a heartbeat, the eyes shift — from violet to gold.

Malachar's voice cuts through: "Foolish. The amulet controls the dragon. Break the amulet, or the dragon kills you both."

How do you free the dragon?
