The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VII - The Amulet's End

Step 7.5 of 8

You ignore the dragon. You ignore Malachar. You charge the Amulet of Domination itself.

Malachar moves — finally — but he's too slow. Your blessed blade (or your bare hands, if you have no other) strikes the purple gem.

The amulet shrieks. A sound like a thousand souls tearing free. Cracks race across its surface. Purple light explodes outward — a nova of liberation.

The dragon's chains shatter. Its eyes blaze gold. It roars — a sound that shakes the fortress foundations. The cage holding your spouse dissolves. They fall into your arms, alive, breathing, yours.

Malachar staggers, his power broken. The fortress begins to crumble. The storm clouds part. Dawn breaks over the Shadowpeaks for the first time in three centuries.

What do you do?
