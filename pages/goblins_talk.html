The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter III - The Forked Tongue

Step 3.5 of 8

You step into the firelight, hands raised, weapon sheathed. The goblins fall silent, then erupt in cruel laughter. Their chieftain — larger, scarred, wearing a crown of bent iron — lumbers forward.

"Talk?" it grunts in the common tongue, thick and mocking. "Pretty human wants to talk. Why?"

You speak of your spouse. Of the dragon. Of the Superlord's tyranny. You offer gold, service, anything for safe passage.

The chieftain's smile reveals filed teeth. "Superlord owns us. Superlord feeds us. Superlord gives power." He gestures to the purple-eye banner. "You offer... nothing."

The spears rise as one. The chieftain laughs as they drive home.

Your final act?
