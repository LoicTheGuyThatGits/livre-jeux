The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Lightless Deep

Step 3 of 8

You run. Deeper. Always deeper. The goblins' shrieks echo behind you, growing distant, then fading. The tunnels twist, branch, descend.

You lose all sense of direction. Time. The darkness is absolute when your torch dies. You navigate by touch — left hand on wall, right hand on sword.

You find things in the deep. Ancient bones — not goblin. Older. Dwarven ruins, crushed by the mountain's weight. A glowing hammer in a stone fist. You take it.

Days pass. Or hours. You drink from underground streams. Eat cave lichen. The hammer glows brighter when goblins are near — a warding weapon.

You emerge from a forgotten mine shaft on the fortress's northern face. The mountain looms above. The hammer sings in your grip.

Where do you go?
