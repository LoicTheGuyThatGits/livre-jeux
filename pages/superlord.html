Chapter VII - The Dark Throne

Step 7 of 8

The throne room of Malachar is a cathedral of darkness. Pillars of black stone rise into shadow, and the air itself seems to pulse with malevolent energy. At the far end of the hall, upon a throne of bones and twisted metal, sits Superlord Malachar himself.

He is not what you expected. He is not a towering monster or a grotesque beast. He is beautiful — terrifyingly so. Pale skin, silver hair, eyes like twin voids that swallow the light. He wears robes of deepest purple, and around his neck hangs the Amulet of Domination — the source of his control over the dragons.

"So," he says, his voice a velvet whisper that somehow fills the entire chamber. "Another hero crawls into my domain. How... delightful."

Behind him, barely visible through the shadows, you can see a massive shape — the dragon, its eyes glowing with the same purple fire as the amulet. And there, in a cage of dark energy beside the throne... your spouse.

This is it. The final confrontation. One way or another, this ends now.

You lunge for the Amulet of Domination — and Malachar does not move at all. He merely raises one pale hand. Purple fire erupts from the flagstones and hangs in the air, weaving itself into a lattice of light: ten columns wide, twenty rows high. A battlefield where flesh is worthless and only cunning matters.

"You wish to challenge my will," he whispers. "Then challenge it properly. Fill twenty lines before I fill mine, and the amulet belongs to you. Lose... and the dragon never flies again."

How do you confront the Superlord?
