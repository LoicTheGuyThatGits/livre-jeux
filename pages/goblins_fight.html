You charge from cover with a warrior's roar, sword flashing in the firelight. The nearest goblins die before they can scream — your blade finds throats and hearts with practiced ease.

But there are many. A goblin shaman raises a staff topped with a purple crystal, and dark energy crackles toward you. You dodge, but the blast catches your shoulder — burning, numbing, poisonous.

You fight through the pain, a whirlwind of steel. The goblins break and flee, shrieking into the night. You stand victorious but wounded, the shaman's poison seeping through your bandages.

The pass is clear. The village beyond the mountains awaits. But your strength wanes with each step.
