The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Amphitheater of Blood

Step 3 of 8

You charge the goblin camp from above, gravity and fury on your side. The first rank dies before they turn. The shaman's purple lightning misses — you're already inside its guard.

But twenty goblins. Twenty. You kill seven before a spear catches your side. Eight before a club shatters your shield. Nine. Ten. The poison in their blades burns hot in your veins.

The chieftain falls last — your sword through its eye. The survivors scatter, shrieking into the mist.

You stand amidst the carnage, bleeding from a dozen wounds. The poison spreads. But the pass is clear. The village — and the mountain beyond — await.

In the chieftain's tent: a map showing a hidden trail up the mountain's east face. And a vial of antidote — goblin-made, foul, but effective.

What do you do?
