The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter V - The Trapped Road

Step 5.5 of 8

The main path is wide, well-trodden, obviously watched. You walk it anyway — speed matters. The fortress draws closer with each step.

Halfway up, the ground shudders. Runes flare beneath your feet — a warding trap left by the Superlord's mages. An enchanted net of cold iron erupts upward, tangling your legs, binding your arms, pinning you to the stone.

You struggle, but the net hums with anti-magic. Your blessed blade (if you have it) cannot cut it. Your strength avails nothing.

Hours later, a patrol finds you. Undead soldiers — empty eyes, mechanical movements. They don't speak. They simply drag you toward the fortress.

The mountain claims another hero. But perhaps... inside the fortress... an opportunity awaits.

What do you do?
