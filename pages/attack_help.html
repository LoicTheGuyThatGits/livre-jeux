The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter I - The Elder's Price

Step 1.5 of 8

You fight through the panicked crowds toward the Elder's hut at the village center. The old woman — Mara — waits at her doorway, staff in hand, unmoved by the chaos.

"You seek the dragon's path," she says, not a question. "I know the old ways. I can read the signs the Superlord left behind. But knowledge has a price."

She demands three things: a lock of your spouse's hair (impossible, they're in the dragon's claws), a vial of dragon's blood (you'd need to wound the beast first), and a promise to return with the Amulet of Domination.

Days pass as you argue, beg, and finally attempt impossible tasks. The trail grows cold. The Elder shakes her head.

"Too late," she murmurs. "The shadows have already claimed what you love."

What now?
