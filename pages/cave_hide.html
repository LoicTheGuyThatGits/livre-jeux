The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter II - The Patient Stone

Step 3 of 8

You extinguish your torch. Press yourself into a niche. Control your breathing. Slow your heart. Become stone.

The goblin horde rushes past — a tide of green skin and crude steel, hunting the intruder who triggered their alarm. They scream past your hiding spot, missing you by inches.

Hours pass. The cave grows quiet. You wait longer. Then slip out, moving opposite their search pattern.

You discover a forgotten shrine deep in the caves — pre-Superlord, dedicated to the Dragon Lords of old. An altar holds a scale — not from Malachar's dragons, but from the First Dragons. It hums with anti-magic.

You take the scale. It will protect you from the amulet's power. You find an exit onto the mountain path — the hidden trail the goblins use.

Where does the hidden trail lead?
