The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter VI - The Rat's Way

Step 6.5 of 8

The hidden passage is a drainage tunnel — three feet wide, slick with slime, reeking of the fortress's waste. You crawl on belly, elbows and knees raw against stone.

Halfway through, the tunnel opens into a cistern chamber. Something large moves in the dark water. Ripples. A low, wet growl.

A sewer beast — pale, eyeless, massive. It rises from the water, tentacles questing. You have seconds.

You throw your torch into the water — oil-slick ignites. The beast shrieks, a sound like tearing metal, and retreats into the depths. You scramble up the far ladder, gasping clean air.

You emerge in a storage basement beneath the throne room. A grate above reveals the Superlord's boots on his dais. He is right there.

How do you strike?
