Chapter II - The Goblin Warren

Step 3 of 8

You draw your sword in the tight confines of the cave. The goblins swarm — dozens of them, screeching, clawing, biting. No room for finesse. Only butchery.

You become a engine of death. Thrust. Parry. Slash. The cave walls run with green blood. The goblin shaman tries its purple magic, but you close the distance and silence it forever.

Hours. It takes hours. The pile of bodies chokes the passage. You are exhausted, wounded, your blade notched and dull.

Finally, silence. The surviving goblins flee deeper into the earth. You find an exit — a fissure opening onto the mountain face. The village lies below.

You descend, barely alive. But you have goblin intel — maps, patrol routes, the location of a secret fortress entrance.
