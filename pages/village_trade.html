The Dragon's Shadow

Chapter IV - The Merchant's Bargain

Step 4.5 of 8

You approach the village smith — a broad-shouldered woman named Brenna with soot-streaked arms and kind eyes. "Work for supplies," you offer. "My arm is strong."

She studies you, then nods. "Three days. Repair the militia's armor, shoe the horses, fix the well pump. Do it well, and you'll leave with a full pack and a blessed blade."

Three days of sweat and hammer-ring. The villagers watch, wary at first, then with grudging respect. Children bring you water. An old man presses a warding charm into your hand — "Against the mountain's evil."

On the third evening, Brenna hands you a sword that hums faintly. "Cold iron, blessed by the old rites. It hurts the undead." She also gives you a hearty pack — food, bandages, climbing gear.

"The hidden trail up the mountain," she whispers. "My grandfather marked it. Few know. Take it."
