Chapter IV - The Cutpurse's End

Step 4.5 of 8

Night falls on the village. You move through shadows, a ghost in the making. The market stalls sit unattended, goods covered by canvas. Easy pickings.

You take dried meat, a fresh waterskin, rope, a whetstone. Supplies for the journey. Your fingers brush a glowing amulet on a jeweler's tray — you pocket that too.

A whistle cuts the air. Superlord's guards — they were waiting. A trap. The "unattended" market was bait.

Crossbow bolts thud into the wood around you. You run, but the village gates are shut. The guards close in, armor gleaming in torchlight.

A cell awaits. The trail goes cold. Your spouse waits in vain.
